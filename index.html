Forge Softwares & Framework Protocol

Effective August 2026
Notice: Engaging Forge Softwares, its sub-brands (Cobra, Runo), or utilizing our autonomous AI workers implies explicit agreement with these standardized engineering and service protocols.

1. Operational Scope & Service Architecture

Forge Softwares acts as a core engineering group executing custom web architecture, autonomous AI sales agents, SaaS platforms, and enterprise CRM pipelines. All deliverables are built according to mutually agreed technical parameters defined during project initialization.

  • Core web platform builds are executed within 7 to 14 business days.
  • Complex autonomous AI agents requiring bespoke LLM fine-tuning require 3 to 4 weeks for full deployment.
  • All code undergoes pre-flight security, SEO, and performance optimization prior to live hosting migration.

2. Financial Protocols & Payment Retainers

Services are billed in accordance with selected package tiers (Starter, Business Pro, Custom Build) or customized statement of work contracts. A mandatory initiation retainer is required to reserve engineering capacity. Remaining balances must be cleared prior to final domain point or repository transfer.

3. Asset Ownership & Intellectual Property

Upon 100% financial settlement, clients retain complete ownership of custom design layouts, domain registration rights, and specific client-side codebases. Forge Softwares retains ownership over underlying reusable framework libraries, proprietary AI agent prompts, and operational backend logic.

4. AI Agent Constraints & Human Escalation

Autonomous AI workers deployed by Forge Softwares operate strictly within the parameters of your business knowledge base. While agents maintain 99.9% operational fidelity, queries exceeding defined domain boundaries are engineered to automatically route to designated human operators.

5. Lifetime Bug Support & System Guarantee

We back our infrastructure with a zero-cost resolution guarantee. If any deployed web system or agent fails to operate according to original project specifications, our engineering team will rectify the discrepancy at no additional expense.

Data Security & Telemetry Standard

Updated August 2026
Forge Softwares enforces bank-grade data privacy standards. We strictly treat your commercial data, lead inputs, and AI worker parameters as strictly confidential assets.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal necessary business telemetries to architect custom platforms and deploy AI sales agents:

  • Contact details submitted via enquiry modals (Company Name, WhatsApp phone numbers, regional location).
  • Operational data inputs required for training bespoke AI agent knowledge bases.
  • Standard web analytics telemetry (browser type, session duration, device viewport) to optimize UX paint speeds.

2. AI Training & Isolation Protocols

Your proprietary client data and internal business communications are completely isolated. Data provided to train your custom AI agents is never used to train global open models or shared across other client infrastructure.

3. Strict Anti-Distribution Guarantee

We do not sell, lease, trade, or expose your personal or enterprise information to third-party ad networks or data brokers. Information transmitted through our Web endpoints (e.g., Google Apps Script pipelines) is strictly routed to our internal project engineering team.

4. Data Erasure & Right to Control

Clients retain full authority over their operational records. You may request complete erasure of project telemetries or contact records from our active servers at any time by contacting our lead engineering team.
